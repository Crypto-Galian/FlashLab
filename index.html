FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are the displayed USDT balances real?

No. They are simulated balances intended for demonstration, UI testing and educational purposes.

Can simulated USDT be withdrawn?

No. Simulated balances have no monetary value and cannot be withdrawn, redeemed or transferred as real cryptocurrency.

Does this create blockchain USDT?

No. The simulator does not mint or alter blockchain USDT.

This generated flash usdt can merge with real balance?

Yes. This “Flash USDT” can legitimately merge with real USDT.

How long does Flash USDT take to expire?

Flash USDT can expire after a period ranging from 6 months up to 365 days.

Why are Flash USDT plans so expensive?

Because the plans cover long-term sustainability, high-quality maintenance, and the resources needed for continuous blockchain development and reliable access.

What networks can be simulated?

The interface supports scenarios for USDT TRC20, USDT ERC20, Bitcoin and TRON.
